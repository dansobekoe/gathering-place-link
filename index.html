Keep it on your phone

Open the portal above, then use your browser’s own Add to Home screen — on Android it is in the ⋮ menu at the top right, and the portal offers an Install button of its own once you are inside.

Do that once and the icon stays. It opens this page’s own address, which never changes, so the app keeps working on the evening the link under it is a new one. Nothing is copied onto your phone: the portal only has anything to show while the laptop running it is switched on and awake.

No account yet?

Register on the portal with your email and phone, and your name will reach the administrator for approval. Nobody is let in on a guess — that is what keeps a class roster private.

Already enrolled and locked out? Use Forgot password on the sign-in page; the portal posts a new code to your own inbox.

This page keeps one address and never changes it. The portal itself runs from a laptop in Accra, so it is only reachable while that laptop is switched on and awake.
